# PRODUCT.md — HIVE RK ENTERPRISES

## Overview
HIVE RK Enterprises is the unified luxury hospitality, nightlife, and bespoke automotive enterprise founded by Rahat Khan in Asansol, West Bengal, delivering elevated culinary, social, and driving experiences.

---

## Target Audience & Personas

### Primary Clientele
- **Discerning Diners & Social Connoisseurs**: Individuals and groups seeking contemporary culinary artistry, artisanal cocktails, and open-air botanical garden ambiance.
- **Private Event & Nightlife Patrons**: Guests desiring intimate velvet-banquette lounge environments, curated soundscapes, and confidential table reservations.
- **Automotive Enthusiasts & VIP Fleet Clients**: Clients seeking certified luxury performance vehicles, exotic superbikes, and white-glove automotive acquisitions.

### Jobs to Be Done
1. **Discover & Explore**: Seamlessly understand the distinct character of each enterprise division (The Lounge RK 2.0, The Lounge RK, HIVE Automobiles).
2. **Reserve & Enquire**: Place private table, lounge buyout, or vehicle consultation requests with immediate confirmation and direct WhatsApp concierge liaison.
3. **Connect with Leadership**: Understand Rahat Khan's founding ethos, heritage commitments, and enterprise vision.

---

## Brand Positioning & Differentiation

### Core Proposition
- Uncompromising British heritage editorial dignity applied to modern luxury hospitality and automotive curation in Eastern India.

### Key Divisions
1. **The Lounge RK 2.0**: Progressive daylight dining, botanical terrace pavilion, artisanal cocktails, and global tapas.
2. **The Lounge RK**: Intimate evening retreat featuring velvet booths, bespoke mixology, and curated soundscapes.
3. **Automobiles & Fleet**: Curated luxury SUVs, exotic motorcycles, and VIP corporate fleet delivery.

---

## Brand Voice & Commitments
- **Tone**: Discreet, authoritative, welcoming, understated luxury.
- **Language**: Editorial restraint without hollow marketing superlatives.
- **Privacy**: Strict concierge confidentiality for all guest enquiries and reservations.

---

## Technical & Accessibility Standards
- **Platform**: Responsive Web (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile).
- **Typography**: Authentic Banham pairings — `IvarHeadline` (Serif display) + `Neue Haas Grotesk Pro 55 Roman` (Grotesk precision).
- **Legibility**: Strict 11px functional UI text floor (`0.6875rem`) and max 65ch reading measure.
- **Accessibility**: Full keyboard `:focus-visible` outlines, Escape dismissals, body scroll-locks on modal triggers, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` fallbacks.
